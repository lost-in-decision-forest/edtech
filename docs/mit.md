# Spotify playlists for MIT Courses
Last Updated: November 1st, 2026

## Blockchain and Money

[MIT – Blockchain and Money (15.S12) – Gary Gensler](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/5ZrpiYqP8nPpTXpujTAg2u) — 13 lectures found on Spotify, Fall 2018

Original course: [Blockchain and Money, 15.S12, MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/15-S12F18)

Note: This one is much better at the original source, although Spotify version does provide some idea if that's not an option. The full course has 24 lectures. On Spotify, Lectures 1, 4-9, 11, and 13-17 were found; Lectures 2, 3, 10, 12, and 18-24 were not found on Spotify, so listen to those on the MIT OpenCourseWare course page linked above. Note also that this course is slide-heavy in places and is not as purely audio-friendly as the Yale lecture courses; it is included as an exception.
